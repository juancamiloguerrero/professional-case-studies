## What these projects represent

Together, these projects show different types of engineering work:

- taking a product from requirements gathering to production and maintenance;
- extending business-critical enterprise modules;
- understanding and transforming existing systems;
- working with APIs, data models, integrations and business rules;
- improving authentication and application security;
- diagnosing issues in document-management workflows;
- building reporting and operational visibility;
- maintaining software where reliability and traceability matter.

## Core technologies across these projects

`Next.js` · `React` · `TypeScript` · `Node.js` · `Express.js` · `MongoDB` · `Mongoose` · `JavaScript` · `EJS` · `REST APIs` · `Firebase` · `AWS / S3-compatible storage` · `ExcelJS` · `JWT` · `Helmet`

Other technologies and integrations are described only where they are relevant to a specific case.

## Engineering approach

**Understand the problem → define the scope → inspect the existing system → design the change → build → validate → harden → evolve.**

I care about software that is not only functional, but also **clear, secure, maintainable and aligned with the actual product and business process**.

## Evidence policy

Screenshots in this repository are included only when they help explain a technical contribution. They are not intended to reproduce entire products or expose internal workflows.

Before publication, every image is reviewed for:

- personal or clinical information;
- customer or applicant data;
- internal URLs, domains and IP addresses;
- credentials, tokens and identifiers;
- confidential logos or client names;
- proprietary infrastructure or business rules.
